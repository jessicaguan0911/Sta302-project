**5. If you can't finish a task on time**

**6. Satisfactory participation**

  submits work of reasonable quality that has been checked (Rmd knits), and reviews teammates' work when asked.
- Contributions are visible through GitHub commits/PRs and are listed in the Contributions section.

**7. If a member does not participate enough**
1. Friendly private message from a teammate.
2. If no change within 3 days: raise it at the next group meeting and agree on a concrete plan.
3. If still unresolved, or no contact for 5+ days: email sta302@course.utoronto.ca
   **at least 5 business days before** the relevant due date, with dates and evidence.
